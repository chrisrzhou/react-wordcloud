# Origin, independence, and attribution

## Project lineage

**Current project:** [Cecil-IA Labs — d3-wordcloud-react](https://github.com/cecil-ia-labs/d3-wordcloud-react); independently maintained open-source React/D3 word-cloud library, developed from an earlier fork.

**Original project:** [Chris Zhou — react-wordcloud](https://github.com/chrisrzhou/react-wordcloud), originally published under the MIT license, with copyright © 2020 Chris Zhou <chrisrzhou@pm.me> in the upstream license file.

## Maintenance responsibility

Cecil-IA Labs and subsequent contributors are responsible for this fork's TypeScript implementation, modernized dependencies and tooling, tests, documentation, behavior changes, CI and releases. The repository has independent maintenance and release decisions. Changes to this fork need not be accepted into or synchronized with the upstream project.

The fork does not claim to originate the upstream architecture or its protected code, and is not endorsed by or officially affiliated with the original maintainers.
